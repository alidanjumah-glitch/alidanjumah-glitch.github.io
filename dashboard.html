Dashboard Tour:

This dashboard starts with the supplied 838-record sales dataset as the demonstration dataset. Download the template, replace the sample rows with your own sales data, save it as Excel/CSV, and upload it. The dashboard validates the schema, confirms upload success/failure, recalculates the KPIs and visuals, and lets you export the result.

How to use your own data

  1. Download Sales Dashboard Template.
  2. Keep the header names unchanged.
  3. Delete the sample rows and paste your own records under the headers.
  4. Save as .xlsx or .csv.
  5. Return here and click Upload Excel / CSV.
  6. Wait for the green Upload successful message, then use filters and visuals.
