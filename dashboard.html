How to use the dashboard:

The supplied 838-record sales file is loaded automatically as demo data. To analyse your own data, download the template, replace the sample records with your own, save as Excel or CSV, upload it, confirm the green upload message, then click Run Analysis.

Workflow

  1. Download Template.
  2. Keep all 12 column headers unchanged.
  3. Paste your sales records under the headers.
  4. Save as .xlsx or .csv.
  5. Upload your file.
  6. Click Run Analysis.

Required headers

TransactionID, Date, CustomerName, ProductID, Quantity, UnitPrice, Region, SalesRep, ProductName, Category, UnitCost, Supplier

Header validation is exact and case-sensitive by column name.
