Executive Biography

Dr. Ali Danjumah Umaru holds a PhD, MSc, and BSc in Economics from Usmanu Danfodiyo University, Sokoto. As the Founder of Caliphate Data Analytics, he leads innovative solutions in data automation and business intelligence. Currently serving as a Manager and Data Analyst at the Statistics Department of the Central Bank of Nigeria (CBN), his career encompasses broad leadership across suevey management, data analytics, currency operations, branch management, branch payment systems, and administrative functions.

As a member of the CBN Internal Faculty, a Full Member of the Nigerian Economic Society (FMNES), and a Fellow of the National Institute of Credit Administration (FICA), Dr. Umaru combines public sector oversight with elite technical proficiency in Power BI, Excel, E-views, Stata, and Microfit.
