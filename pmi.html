Monthly PMI Analytical Table

CBN/MPC analytical template • PMI interpretation: >50 expansion, 50 no change, <50 contraction. Supplier delivery is direction-adjusted: faster delivery raises the index. Sector methodology follows the CBN reporting structure; see methodology note below.
Methodology: Industry PMI: Output 25%, New Orders 30%, Supplier Delivery 15%, Employment 20%, Raw Materials/Inventory 10%. Services and Agriculture: Output, New Orders, Employment and Raw Materials/Inventory at 25% each. Composite PMI: Output 25%, New Orders 30%, Employment 20%, Raw Materials/Inventory 10% and Supplier Delivery Time 15%; Supplier Delivery Time is calculated only for Industry and included in the Composite. Services and Agriculture do not have Supplier Delivery Time and use their four available components equally. The additional survey variables — Export Orders, Import Orders, Average Output Price, Average Input Price, Quantity Purchased, Outstanding Business and Finished Goods — are separate diffusion indices and are not added to PMI. Average Output Price and Average Input Price are converted from Higher/Same/Lower responses to diffusion percentages and displayed to one decimal place. Average Input and Output Prices are juxtaposed with New Orders to distinguish demand-led from cost-push/pass-through pressure; the Input-Output gap is a cost-absorption diagnostic.
